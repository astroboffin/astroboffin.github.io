This directory contains material related to the paper published in Science, 9
November 2012, pp. 773-775:
"An Interacting Binary System Powers Precessing Outflows of an Evolved Star"
by 
Henri M. J. Boffin, Brent Miszalski, Thomas Rauch, David Jones, Romano
 L. M. Corradi, Ralf Napiwotzki, Avril C. Day-Jones, and Joachim Koeppen

Boffin_Science_final.pdf is the unformatted paper (PDF)

Fg1_SupMat.pdf contains the Supplementary information (PDF)

1225386fig1.eps, 1225386fig2.eps and 1225386fig3.eps are the figures from the
paper (Postscript)

fg1.tar.gz (15 MB) is a gzipped tar file that contains the individual fits files used for making Fig. 2 of the
paper, as well as the ESO Press Photo 1244a (see
http://www.eso.org/public/images/eso1244a/)

Fg1data.tar.gz (146 MB!) is a gzipped tar file containing the raw observations
of Fg 1 done at SAAO in March 2010, through 2 bands: I and U. Calibrations
files, bias and flats, are also included.

fg1phot_red.tar.gz (354 MB) is a gzipped tar file containing the resulting 
processed observations, from which the photometric data points were measured.

For additional information, please contact Henri Boffin (hboffin AT eso.org)
