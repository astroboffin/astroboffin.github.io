ETHOS 1

 
 
 
 
 

Discovery image of ETHOS 1 made from I (red), R (green) and B (blue) POSS-II SuperCOSMOS Sky Survey images. The turquoise hue is typical of PNe in this particular colour-composite image combination. North is to top and East to left.


Credit and Copyright: B. Miszalski, R. Corradi, H. Boffin et al.
